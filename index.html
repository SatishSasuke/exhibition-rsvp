The Exhibition

A codex carved from stone, light, and current, The Living Lab traces the classical elements through mineral specimens, immersive installations, and a digital twin revealing the strata hidden beneath every stone.

Each chamber operates as a self-contained study across three pillars: Ecological, Elemental, and Resonant. Guests navigate the circuit at their own pace, guided by hairline maps and quiet light.

Before You Arrive

Frequently Asked

Is entry free?

Yes. The Living Lab is offered without charge; a reservation simply secures your passage through each elemental chamber.

Who may attend?

The exhibition welcomes all ages. Guests under 12 should be accompanied by an adult throughout the circuit.

Do children need their own reservation?

Please reserve one slot for every visitor in your party, including children, so we can pace each chamber comfortably.

Is photography permitted?

Photography for personal use is welcomed. We ask that flash be silenced near the crystalline installations.

What should I bring?

Only your curiosity. Comfortable footwear is recommended for the full circuit through all five elements.
